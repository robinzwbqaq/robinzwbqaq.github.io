01 About

My work asks a simple question: how can we make generative models more capable, reliable, and efficient?

I develop learning methods across programming languages, natural language, and visual generation. My research spans learnable representations for code, linguistic signals for AI-text detection, and parameter-efficient diffusion model distillation.

I care about ideas that are mathematically grounded, empirically rigorous, and useful in real systems. I am always open to conversations and research collaborations in these areas.
